# RESEARCH.md — Drylining Maidstone

## Local Market

Maidstone is the county town of Kent, on the River Medway, midway between London (32 mi NW) and the Channel ports. Population 109,490 (2021 census). Property stock is mixed: Georgian and Victorian terraces in the town centre, Edwardian semis (Bearsted), 1930s semis (Loose, Allington), post-war council estates (Harrietsham, Staplehurst), and continuous new-build estate development on the village edges (Boxley, Lenham). Two conservation areas in the borough centre plus several village conservation cores (Yalding, Loose, East Farleigh riverside, parts of Bearsted).

M20 and M2 motorways converge near Maidstone. Three rail stations: Maidstone East, Maidstone West, Maidstone Barracks. Cross-country services to Ashford International, Strood, and London Victoria.

Main NHS site: Maidstone Hospital (MTW NHS Trust). County Hall is KCC HQ on Sandling Road. Fremlin Walk and Sessions Square are the main retail centres. Lockmeadow is the riverside entertainment/market complex — the only Lockmeadow-named site in Maidstone, NOT a residential district.

Local authority: Maidstone Borough Council (planning authority). Building Regs 2010 (England): Part B (Fire Safety), Part E (Resistance to Sound), Part L (Thermal). BS 8212 governs drylining; BS EN 520 plasterboard.

## Cost Data

£/m² rates in `docs/research/cost-guide-sources.md` (per 2026-09 build):
- Standard 12.5mm plasterboard (dot-and-dab): £25–£42/m²
- Tape-and-joint finish: £7–£11/m²
- Full skim: £7–£11/m²
- Moisture-resistant/fire-rated upgrade: +£5–£9/m²
- Insulated plasterboard (PIR 50–80mm): £38–£60/m²
- Acoustic system: £45–£72/m²
- Suspended ceiling (MF): £28–£50/m²
- Metal stud partition (single layer): £55–£100/m
- Metal stud partition (double, acoustic/fire): £78–£135/m

Kent labour day rates: £200–£280 (5–15% above UK average).
